## Analysis sequence

1. Corpus and observability audit
2. Emotional repertoire
3. Appraisal and communication-function structure
4. Temporal emotional arc
5. Organizational-role comparison
6. Website/social platform comparison
7. Matched-message adaptation analysis
8. Engagement / affective uptake
9. Public interaction analysis
10. Affective circulation
11. Affective conversion
12. Integrated affective-intermediation analysis

## Primary conceptual chain

`appraisal → organizational emotion/function → action orientation → public uptake → circulation → conversion`

## Required robustness checks

- strict eligible only vs eligible + probably eligible
- item-weighted vs organization-normalized estimates
- website-only temporal benchmark
- day-precision-only weekly analyses
- high-confidence matched sets only
- alternative overall-emotion operationalizations
- leave-one-high-volume-organization-out checks

## Interpretation guardrails

- Do not equate communication volume with public salience.
- Do not equate engagement with emotion.
- Do not interpret missing social posts as silence.
- Do not infer public emotion from organization-authored content.
- Do not compare minimum-observed repost traces with official share totals as equivalent metrics.
- Do not make causal platform claims from unmatched descriptive differences.
- Do not treat visible commenters as representative of the broader public.
