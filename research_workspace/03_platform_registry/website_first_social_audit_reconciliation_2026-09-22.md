## Important interpretation rule

`website checked` or `channel verified` is not the same as `historical study-window archive complete`.

1. organizational eligibility;

4. individual post verification;

A zero retrieved count from Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, or YouTube must not be interpreted as organizational silence when historical retrieval is incomplete.
